# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and technical. Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Keep discussions on topic and provide reproducible details when reporting defects.
- Respect privacy: never post real invoices, contracts, identity documents, or other sensitive files.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, security, and release timing.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks
- Publishing another person's private information
- Uploading malware, exploits against third parties, or sensitive user documents
- Spam, undisclosed commercial promotion, or repeated off-topic messages

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation for violations. Report conduct issues privately through the repository maintainer contact listed on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and kept confidential where possible.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
